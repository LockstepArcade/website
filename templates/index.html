About Lockstep Arcade

Lockstep Arcade is in active development. Three games are available, currently.

Neon Swarm

A retake on the classic "Lemmings" puzzle-strategy game, reimagined for competitive and cooperative multiplayer! Guide your lemmings from start to exit, managing limited resources and competing against up to 3 other players.

Pilots

Fast paced battles between spaceships in a top down view with smooth inertial movement, based on target locking and homing missiles.

Serpents

Combination of the classic 'Snake' game with inertial (asteroids style) movement.

If you're here to beta test the game, check out the testing challenges.
